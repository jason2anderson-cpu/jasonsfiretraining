EMERGENCY — THE SEQUENCE

You lose water on an attack line. One drilled sequence, top to bottom.

1

EMERGENCY IDLE

2

NOTIFY COMMAND

The pump work comes first. Prime, close what needs closing, open tank to pump — that is what keeps the interior crew alive. Notifying Command is a later priority than restoring water.

WORK BACKWARDS TO CAB

IF THE SUPPLY CAVITATES

If the water supply cavitates and pulls a vacuum — CLOSE THE SUPPLY, then OPEN TANK TO PUMP.

Separate response, not part of the drilled sequence — this is what you do the moment it happens.

MINIMUM INTAKE PRESSURE

20 PSI

Maintain 20 PSI intake pressure.

20 psi minimum protects from drawing a vacuum on the intake hose.

WHAT THE OREM MANUAL SAYS

The Engine Company Operations manual contains no loss-of-water, cavitation, or pump-overheat troubleshooting procedure. Its one statement touching supply failure is on the back-up line — the situations where the back-up line should be used:

  • An additional attack line is needed.
  • Pressure loss to the attack line.
  • Attack team is trapped by fire.
  • Search team members egress is cut off.

Engine Company Operations 2023 V-1, Back-Up Line, 4-7
