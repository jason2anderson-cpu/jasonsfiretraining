Roof operations three separate operations, never mixed

R1Residential — walkableCrew of 2 · single ladder — the Engineer is usually first: sounds, passes the hook back, takes the sawR2Residential — steep pitchCrew of 2 · same single-ladder patternR3CommercialCrew of 3 or more · Lead Hook goes first and sounds; the Engineer makes the inspection cut

Engineer = Lead Saw — fixed. Lead Hook and every other role go to whoever is on the crew that day. OFD Truck Manual v3.1.
