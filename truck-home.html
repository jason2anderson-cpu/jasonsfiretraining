STOP — COMMAND CONFIRMATION REQUIRED

Confirm all department members are out of the building before flowing the master stream.

Affirmative acknowledgement, not a one-way notification. A master stream must be shut down to be repositioned — the safest positions are the building corners; the front is dangerous, ineffective, and inside the collapse zone.

Engine Company Operations 2023 V-1, p.133 (7-1)

ROOF OPERATIONS

Three separate operations. Never mixed.

R1
Residential — walkable

Crew of 2 · single ladder — the Engineer is usually first: sounds, passes the hook back, takes the saw · drilled in aerial evolution 1.0

R2
Residential — steep pitch

Crew of 2 · same single-ladder pattern · drilled in aerial evolution 1.1

R3
Commercial

Crew of 3 or more · Lead Hook goes first and sounds; the Engineer makes the inspection cut · drilled in aerial evolution 1.2

Engineer = Lead Saw — fixed. Lead Hook and every other role go to whoever is on the crew that day. OFD Truck Manual v3.1 — the Engineer is the Lead Saw and leads the roof operation. Crew sizes: Truck Manual and ADO-Aerial Task Book, evolutions 1.0–1.2.
